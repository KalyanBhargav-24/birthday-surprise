# Bondam — The Ultimate Interactive Birthday Experience 🥥🎂

An extraordinary, cinematic, funny, and emotional birthday surprise website created for **BONDAM**, built with HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.

## 🌟 The Cinematic Story Sequence

1. **🌴 Tropical Coconut Tree Opening Scene**:
   - Majestic coconut tree with swaying palm leaves, textured trunk, tropical sky with drifting clouds, and hanging coconuts.
2. **🥥 Branch Detachment & Gravity Drop**:
   - A chosen coconut attached to the tree wiggles and visibly detaches from its branch, accelerating down with physics-based gravity, angled arc, and rotation.
3. **💥 Ground Impact & Shell Fracture**:
   - Shakes the screen on impact, spawns dust and leaf particles, bounces twice with squash-and-stretch, and cracks into two halves with confetti and sparkles.
4. **📸 Image 6 Surprise Reveal**:
   - Bondam's original photo drinking from a coconut appears in a glowing Polaroid frame with Telugu dialogue:
     > *"అయ్యో బొండం వచ్చేసింది! 😂"*
     > *"ఇన్ని రోజులు దాచిన సర్ప్రైజ్ ఈరోజే బయటపడింది! ❤️"*
5. **🎉 Hero Birthday Celebration**:
   - Unlocked by clicking *"సర్ప్రైజ్ ఓపెన్ చేయ్ ❤️"*.
   - Features Telugu headings, floating balloons, party ribbons, and grand quotes.
6. **😂 Funny Telugu BONDAM Comedy Zone**:
   - 5 interactive dialogue cards with micro-animations.
   - Upgraded *Tingaritanam Analysis Lab* with animated personality stats.
7. **🎂 Interactive Birthday Cake**:
   - 3D styled cake with candles and flickering flames.
   - Clicking *"Make a Wish ✨"* extinguishes candles with smoke particles, celebration fanfare, and reveals a heartwarming birthday blessing.
8. **💌 Special Birthday Letter in 3D Envelope**:
   - Clicking the vintage wax-sealed envelope unfolds the flap and slides out an emotional Telugu letter with floating hearts.
9. **📸 Memories Gallery (Images 1 to 5)**:
   - Displays all 5 original memories in strict sequential order in scrapbook Polaroid frames.
   - Tap any photo to open full-resolution uncropped Lightbox.
10. **🌟 Final Surprise & Adventure Replay**:
    - Final emotional message with *"మళ్ళీ మొదటికి వెళ్దాం! ❤️"* to seamlessly replay the entire experience from the start.

## 🎵 Built-in Web Audio & Sound Engine
- Integrated Web Audio API synthesizer: zero audio file dependencies required!
- Realistic sound effects: falling swoosh, heavy thud impact, wood crack snap, candle blow breath, celebration fanfare, and gentle ambient background melody.
- MP3 fallback supported in `assets/audio/`.
- Mute/Unmute control at top-right.

## 🚀 How to Run
Open `index.html` directly in your browser or run a local server:
```bash
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8080`!
